## Diagnosis

1. Half the site is not indexed; stale noindex verdicts and never-crawled URLs.
2. The largest page targets calendar-assistant intent, not phone-agent intent.
3. Money pages are not built for their terms: the receptionist hub does not rank for "ai receptionist" (49.5K/month, KD 25); the homepage title and H1 carry no category term; cost, software, dental, medical and law-firm terms (KD 3–22) have no dedicated page.
4. Measurement is blind: the consent gate hides ~90% of sessions and no engagement key events exist.
5. Thin hubs and sparse cross-links (728-word homepage; 1,218-word flagship post with four internal links).
6. Near-zero authority relative to competitors.

## What changed in this worktree (local, uncommitted until reviewed)

Worktree `seo-phase-0` is based on `7576139` (origin/main including the property-management program, PR #218); the local `main` checkout was still at `cad6278` when this was written.

- `apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts`: the 35 static routes now carry `lastModified` from `apps/web/data/static-page-dates.mjs`; the root URL is emitted as `https://quickvoice.co/`, the form Search Console reports as the homepage canonical (Next's metadata resolver strips the slash from the page's own canonical tag; the two forms are URL-equivalent). `apps/web/data/static-page-sources.mjs` maps each route to its source files and `scripts/update-static-page-dates.mjs` regenerates the dates from commit history (`--check` reports staleness). Run it after committing a change that touches static pages, then commit the regenerated file; CI shallow clones cannot run `--check` without full history.
- `apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx`: removed the ignored `keywords` metadata. `apps/web/src/app/open-source/page.tsx`: absolute canonical and `og:url` like every other page. The homepage canonical was left as is: a trailing-slash value is collapsed by Next at render time, so changing it has no effect.
- `apps/web/src/lib/blog-schedule.mjs` + `scripts/check-seo-campaign.mjs`: CI now fails when a non-draft article is past its publication date without a valid evidence review (live for readers, noindex for search), and prints a warning for articles due within 7 days. Articles are scheduled weekly, so a hard failure on the 7-day window would have kept CI red by the calendar alone. Next due: `questions-before-buying-ai-voice-agent-platform` (2026-10-12).
- Evidence reviews stamped for `quickvoice-vs-retell-ai`, `case-study-call-center-cost-reduction-ai-voice` and `state-of-ai-voice-agents-2026` after checking their cited sources (Retell quickstart, Vapi phone quickstart, Bland pathways, NIST AI RMF fetched live; README project-status lines and `apps/ai/handlers/mcp_handler.py` verified in the public repository). Reviewer recorded as "Claude (primary-source and repository review)".
- `apps/web/public/llms.txt`: refreshed (date, Qdrant as configurable vector store, hosted pricing facts, solution/use-case/industry/pricing/docs pages, comparison pages, scenario caveat).
- `apps/web/data/blog-journeys.mjs`: one journey added (`quickvoice-vs-synthflow`), the only candidate whose body already links to its hub, pricing and contact pages; the other comparison and industry guides need body-level links and a re-review and are scheduled with the Phase 1 content work.
- Tests: `apps/web/tests/seo-publishing.test.mjs` (static lastmod, root URL, source-map coverage, absolute canonicals, no keywords tag), `apps/web/tests/blog-schedule.test.mjs`.
- `docs/marketing/seo/inventory.csv`: the three reviewed articles' rows updated to the reviewed status.
