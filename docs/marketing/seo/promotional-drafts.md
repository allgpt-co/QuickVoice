## Post 01: Small-business owner

Free scheduling software can be useful, but start with the job you need it to do. A booking link, a reminder and a conversation with a caller are different workflows. Before choosing, write down who updates the calendar, how changes are handled and who takes over when the system cannot complete a request. Our guide helps you compare those requirements before committing to a tool.
