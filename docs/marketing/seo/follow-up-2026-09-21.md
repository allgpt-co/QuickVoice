## Bounded repair

- Analytics is off until explicit opt-in. No Google tag or collection request is
  initiated by this integration before consent. Advertising consent stays off.
- Accessible, equally presented Allow analytics / Decline analytics buttons,
  a persistent Privacy choices button, keyboard focus on reopening and focus
  return after choosing. The controls do not require completing a form.
- Only the preference is saved in local storage. Missing, invalid or unavailable
  storage defaults off. Revocation blocks custom and automatic collection,
  clears accessible GA cookies, and updates other open tabs. Forms stay intact.
- Optional enquiry context is captured after consent, omitted when declined,
  and discarded on revocation. Form contact fields and receipts stay out of the
  custom lead event. Pre-consent submissions are not replayed after opt-in.
- Unconditional TruConversion loading is removed. Its account-level input
  masking and a reliable revocation control have not been verified; the recorder
  stays disabled. This is not a claim that previously collected data was deleted.
- Browser and API success messages say submitted. Provider acknowledgement is
  not proof of recipient inbox delivery, booking, qualification or revenue.

Google's [basic consent behavior](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10000067)
and [collection-disable control](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/privacy)
inform the implementation. TruConversion documents that input obfuscation is
an [account privacy option](https://help.truconversion.com/knowledgebase/privacy-options/);
its defaults are not sufficient evidence of this site's masking configuration.
