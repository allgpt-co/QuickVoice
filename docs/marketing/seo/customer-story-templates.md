# Three customer-story briefs and evidence requests

Prepared templates only. No verified QuickVoice customer outcomes were located in the bounded Drive search. All fields below are intentionally blank; none of the 33 existing illustrative scenarios supplies customer evidence. Do not publish a template as a finished story or infer a testimonial from a support conversation.

For each story, keep raw records in the customer's approved evidence system. This repository should contain only approved aggregate results and public attribution. Track a source reference, evidence owner, measurement period, deployed version/scope, exact approved wording and customer permission for every public claim.
