# How to use the distribution research

[The CSV](distribution-opportunities.csv) contains 20 distinct outlets checked against primary publisher/platform pages on 2026-09-06. “Verified” means the outlet and linked public participation route/rules were observed; it does not mean QuickVoice is eligible, an authenticated form was tested, an editor agreed, or a backlink exists. Every row is `researched_not_contacted`.

These are audience-fit opportunities, not a list of links to buy. The relevant destination column identifies the associated resource for internal planning. It does **not** instruct an editor to place that URL where their rules prohibit promotional links. CMSWire, Reworked, HubSpot's agency guidelines and other editorial outlets expect useful expertise; author attribution or contextual links remain their decision.

Before contacting an outlet, check for an existing QuickVoice entry, recheck its current rules, satisfy its stated eligibility, choose a real accountable author/submitter and obtain the already-required authorization to send. No messages, account creation, submissions, payments, review requests or public posts were performed in preparing this package.

## Specific boundaries discovered

- [DZone](https://dzone.com/articles/dzones-article-submission-guidelines) prohibits even partly AI-drafted articles. The CSV supplies a topic brief only; a practitioner must write any proposed article independently from their real work.
- [CustomerThink](https://customerthink.com/editorial-standards/) requires the named human author to draft the post. Research/ideation can inform their work; these promotional drafts are not acceptable submissions.
- [DEV](https://dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev) allows disclosed, checked AI assistance but prohibits business promotion in AI-assisted articles. Do not post the owned-channel promotional copy there or generate community comments.
- [LiveKit Community](https://community.livekit.io/guidelines) permits promotion only in designated channels such as show-and-tell. Do not promote in support threads or contact members by unsolicited DM.
- [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/contributions/) says its guest-byline program is paused. It was researched and **excluded** from the 20 opportunities; a sponsored alternative is not implicitly authorized.
- SaaSHub's public instructions were available, but the direct product form returned an access error. Its row records this limitation; the actual account form must be inspected before submission.
- [OpenSourceAlternative.to](https://opensourcealternative.to/submit) and [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) currently describe long standard review queues. Do not make directory publication a guaranteed Day-90 milestone.

## Record outcomes without inventing them

After an authorized action, set `sent_date`, retain the exact submission/pitch and update status to `submitted`, `awaiting_editor`, `declined`, `published`, or `held` with a specific reason. Record the actual published URL and observed link destination/attributes if publication occurs. Keep private account details and correspondence out of the public repository. Report replies, qualified referral sessions and enquiries separately from raw placement counts.

Use a single relevant pitch per outlet. Follow up only if their policy permits it and sending is authorized. Do not solicit manufactured reviews, trade irrelevant links, send mass pitches, or treat an API integration as an approved partner relationship.
