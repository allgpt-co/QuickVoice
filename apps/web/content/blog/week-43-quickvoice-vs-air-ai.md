Direct attempts to verify the older voice-service documentation at `docs.air.ai/introduction` and `docs.air.ai/api-reference/introduction` failed. Search-engine copies of older pages do not establish a currently available service, its legal identity, or its terms.

This observation does not establish whether the earlier service has ceased operating, moved domains, or changed ownership. No such conclusion is asserted. It does mean that the current domain cannot responsibly be used to substantiate the earlier voice-product comparison.

Before reconsidering publication, the editor must verify the exact company and product, a working official documentation source, current account/service availability, relevant contract terms, and a reproducible workflow evaluation.
